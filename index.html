About Bannockburn

Population

Approximately 8,500 residents

Location

Cook County, Illinois - North Shore region

Founded

1958 as a planned community

Community Spirit

Known for excellent schools and vibrant community engagement

Bannockburn is a charming residential village on Chicago's North Shore, known for its excellent schools, beautiful parks, and strong sense of community. Home to diverse families and professionals, Bannockburn offers a unique blend of suburban tranquility with easy access to Chicago's cultural and economic opportunities.
